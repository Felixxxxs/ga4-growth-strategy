# From data to a marketing and sales strategy

## 1. Diagnosis first

One sentence, chosen from the evidence:

- **Traffic problem:** the funnel converts at or above good practice, there just are not enough qualified visitors.
- **Conversion-infrastructure problem:** visitors arrive but leak at a step (no lead capture, weak offer page, broken checkout, no booking path).
- **Follow-through problem:** leads arrive but nobody nurtures, calls back or recovers them.

Most small businesses have the second or third. The default advice then: do not widen the top until the middle and bottom stop leaking.

## 2. Channel scorecard

List every channel the business uses AND the relevant ones it does not.

| # | Channel | Status | Evidence (12 months) | Funnel role | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Status: active · idle · stopped without replacement · built but not deployed · never used.
Evidence: sessions, first users, key events, purchases, revenue per session, cost if paid.
Verdict: keep and restructure · defend · build now · repurpose only · stop · later.

## 3. Funnel roles

| Stage | Job | Typical channels | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Be found by the right persona | SEO articles, AI answer visibility, social, video, PR, partners, communities, directories | First users, engaged sessions in the clean market, e-mail captures |
| Consideration | Earn trust and a next step | Lead magnet, quiz or assessment, webinar, e-mail and messaging sequences (8 to 12 touches over 60 to 90 days), retargeting, comparison pages | Leads in a sequence, booked calls |
| Decision | Remove risk and close | Money pages, commercial and local SEO, precise paid search, instant booking, checkout optimisation, reviews | Calls held, checkout completion, orders |
| Retention | Deliver, expand, get referred | Onboarding, next-product ladder, reviews at success moments, referral programme | Repeat rate, reviews, referral sales |

The measurement chain the strategy is steered by: sessions → leads → booked calls → held → customers, and in parallel sessions → checkout started → purchase.

## 4. Gap analysis

Rate each dimension against good practice for this business type: CRITICAL · HIGH · MEDIUM · fine.

Dimensions: funnel endpoint (what the visitor can do next), lead magnet, nurture, booking and speed-to-lead, offer page, checkout, paid search precision, retargeting, local presence, AI answer visibility, partnerships, webinars or events, reviews, referral, positioning vs competitors, price ladder (is there an entry product?).

Offer-side gaps that no channel can fix (price jumps, no entry product, unclear guarantee) go in their own list.

## 5. Prioritisation: fix the leaks, then the free channels, then buy traffic

- **Tier 1, this week:** days of effort, no new budget. Repair measurement, pause dead spend, fix the funnel endpoint or booking path, deploy assets that already exist, reconnect nurture, recover failed payments.
- **Tier 2, this month:** paid restructure, reconciliation routine, money-page SEO, referral and review asks, messaging channel, directories, checkout sprint.
- **Tier 3, this quarter:** webinars, partnerships, comparison pages, AI-visibility programme, retargeting, social repurposing, B2B.

Every item: why now, owner, effort, measure, and value **in units of the core product** ("one extra sale per week of product X = +N per year"). Backend-quantified leaks rank before A/B tests.

## 6. Budget logic

- Stop campaigns with zero leads and purchases in 12 months.
- Move spend from clusters with high cost per key event to clusters with low, and state the expected gain.
- No new paid channel (prospecting social, Performance Max, Display, YouTube) until reconciliation proves unit economics and the conversion signal is clean. Value-based bidding on broken tracking burns money invisibly.
- Paid only pays when the average order clears the break-even cost per sale.
- Retargeting starts when audiences exceed about 100 users (more for some platforms).
- New tests run capped (fixed amount over 4 weeks) with a decision date.

## 7. Sales-side actions

- Instrument the call path: booked and held events, CRM logging, instant calendar booking behind a short qualifier, speed-to-lead target, reminders against no-shows.
- Assessment or quiz results route into booking.
- Abandoned checkout and failed payment recovery within the hour.
- Wallet payments prominent on mobile.
- Next-product sequence for existing customers; review and referral asks at success moments.
- Measure lead-to-customer rate against a benchmark; capture residence or company size where it decides fit.

Retargeting audiences that usually matter: checkout started and not purchased (30 days), pricing viewed and no checkout, engaged researchers, existing leads (exclusion or upsell).

## 8. What not to do (default list, extend per business)

- Widen the top of the funnel while the middle leaks.
- Judge channels on 28 days of purchases.
- Kill a channel that starts journeys because last click shows nothing.
- Add paid channels before the conversion signal is clean.
- Read Direct as a channel.
- Extrapolate a one-off spike.
- Chase back lost informational traffic that never bought.

## 9. KPIs and cadence

| KPI | Today (backend-corrected) | 90-day target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

Typical rows: booked calls per month, checkout completion rate, orders per month, share of leads in a nurture sequence, key event rate of organic sessions in the clean market, reviews, referral sales.

Cadence: monthly review (`monthly` mode, together with the Ads and Search Console pulls), ad hoc pulls per campaign or test, reconciliation after every tracking fix, targets revisited after the first month of clean data, full strategy every quarter.
